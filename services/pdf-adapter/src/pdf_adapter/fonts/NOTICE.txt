Bundled font files in this directory ship with the pdf-adapter package
as fallback fonts for the PDF exporter. They are not authored by the
AllDocs project; they are redistributed verbatim under their original
license.

    Source: Google Fonts (https://fonts.google.com).

Each TTF here is the Latin-only subset published by the fontsource
project (https://fontsource.org) under the same Apache 2.0 license.

These fonts are metric-compatible with the common Microsoft fonts they
replace (Arimo↔Arial, Tinos↔Times New Roman, Cousine↔Courier New) — the
same property Liberation Sans/Serif/Mono target. They are used in
preference to PDF base-14 stock fonts when an extracted subset from a
source PDF can't be reused.
